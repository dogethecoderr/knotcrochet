How to report a copyright concern (DMCA).

Last updated 13 November 2026.

Everything on this site is our own work: we host no content from other users. If you still believe something here infringes your copyright, tell us and we will look at it promptly.

How to send a notice

Send a written notice to our designated agent with:

  1. Your name, address, phone number and email.
  2. The copyrighted work you say was infringed.
  3. The page address of the material you want removed.
  4. A statement that you believe in good faith the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the notice is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner.
  6. Your physical or electronic signature.
