Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site.

Last updated 13 November 2026.

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we have done

  • Pages use headings, landmarks and lists so screen readers can find their way around.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Photographs have text descriptions.
  • Text and controls meet AA colour contrast, and the layout works at 320 pixels wide and at 200% zoom.
  • Motion is switched off for visitors who ask their device to reduce it.

Known limits

The site was checked with automated tools and by keyboard. It has not yet had a full audit with screen reader users.

Tell us about a problem

If something is hard to use, email [CONTACT EMAIL] with the page and what went wrong. We aim to reply within 5 working days.
