How Knot Crochet Co. handles personal information.

Last updated 13 November 2026.

Knot Crochet Co. is a small handmade crochet shop run by a student, with [OPERATOR LEGAL NAME] (a parent or guardian) as the adult responsible for this website. Questions go to [CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

Right now this site has no accounts, no forms, no working checkout and no analytics. We do not ask for your name, email, address or payment details, and we do not sell or share personal information.

  • Server logs. Our host, [HOSTING PROVIDER], may record your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, as every web host does, to keep the site running and secure.
  • Fonts. The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google’s servers and Google receives your IP address. See Google’s privacy policy for how it handles that.
  • Emails you send us. If you write to us, we keep your message and address only to reply.

Cookies and local storage

We set no cookies. We store two items in your browser’s local storage: your cookie choice, so we do not ask again, and the contents of your cart. Neither is sent to us. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

Payments

You can add items to a cart, but checkout does not take orders or payment yet. Before checkout opens we will update this policy to name the payment processor and what it collects.

How long we keep things

Emails are deleted within [RETENTION PERIOD] of our last reply. Host logs follow the host’s own schedule. Your cookie choice stays in your browser until you clear it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the EEA and UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA), you may ask to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information we hold about you, object to its use, and opt out of its sale or sharing. We do not sell or share personal information, and we will not treat you differently for making a request. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will answer within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children’s privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (COPPA) or under 16 in the EEA. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will post any changes here with a new date.
