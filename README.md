# Knot Crochet Co.

**Visit the site: <https://dogethecoderr.github.io/knotcrochet/>**

I made this website for my sister Aarna and her small crochet business, **Knot Crochet Co.** She makes everything by hand: checkered handbags, turtle plushies, mini loaf cats, strawberry keychains and scrunchies.

## What's on it

- **Home**: a crochet hook stitches a frame around the headline, then the creations, a bit about Aarna, and her upcoming events.
- **Shop**: every creation with its photo, price and description.
- **Cart**: add things you like and see the total.

Online checkout isn't open yet, so nothing can be ordered or paid for on the site. For now, come find her at her next event.
